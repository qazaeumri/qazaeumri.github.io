A few helpful details

Where can I download the app?

The app is coming soon. The Home page will link to the App Store and Google Play when downloads are available.

Are my records synced to the cloud?

No. The current app stores records locally and has no account or cloud sync. JSON export lets you deliberately save a backup through your device. Device backups are separate; see the Privacy Policy.

Can you recover or delete my local records?

We cannot access or recover records stored only on your device. You can edit individual entries or use full reset to clear the app’s local records and recovery history. Delete exported or device-backup copies separately where they are stored.

Can support answer religious or medical questions?

We help with the app. QAZA E UMRI does not provide religious rulings or medical advice. Ask a qualified scholar about religious obligations and a qualified healthcare professional about health concerns.
